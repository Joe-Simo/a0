A0 dense: reply bare lines `fn f ...` (whole function, adds or replaces), `-fn f`. Bodies are prefix, fixed arity: `add mul A B C` = A*B+C; params A B C; last statement is the result. Ops: add sub mul div rem and or xor << >> select eq ne lt le gt ge(->bool) get set at put fold F n s a..: s=F(s,i,a..); [a b] array, (a b) record. Types only if not all u32: `fn f u32x4 u32 -> bool`.
