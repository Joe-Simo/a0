a call putnum p0 ip

dot text "."

hq div fp 100
hundreds rem hq 10
tq div fp 10
tens rem tq 10
ones rem fp 10
d0 add thousands 48
d1 add hundreds 48
d2 add tens 48
d3 add ones 48

end
