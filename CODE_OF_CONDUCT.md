# Code of Conduct

A0 is a small research project. We want it to be a place where anyone can ask a question, report a failure or argue about a measurement without being
made to feel unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific and honest. A bug report with a minimal program, a measurement with its file and its load, and a loss reported as a loss are the most
  useful things you can bring.
- Credit other people's work, and say when you do not know something.
- Keep discussion on the project: the language, the compiler, the tools, the measurements.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing other people's private information, and spam or promotion unrelated to the project.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space run for the project. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close
threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue that avoids naming a person where possible, or use GitHub's
"report content" and "block user" tools; a maintainer will look at it and reply on the thread or privately where GitHub allows.

This text is short on purpose. If something it does not cover makes the project worse for people, say so and we will adjust it.
